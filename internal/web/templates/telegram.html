Two independent lanes. Upload covers everything that isn't a download (SaveFilePart, SaveBigFilePart, SendMedia, journal posts, snapshot uploads, channel scans). Download covers upload.getFile only (cache misses + range reads). The split keeps a heavy upload session from starving reads. The running daemon polls these settings every 5 s — changes take effect without a remount, whether you set them here or via telfs ratelimit set.
