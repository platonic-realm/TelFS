Request rate limits

Two independent lanes. Upload covers everything that isn't a download (SaveFilePart, SaveBigFilePart, SendMedia, journal posts, snapshot uploads, channel scans). Download covers upload.getFile only (cache misses + range reads). The split keeps a heavy upload session from starving reads.
The upload lane is adaptive by default — when no explicit cap is set, the daemon halves the cap on every observed FLOOD_WAIT and slowly raises it during quiet windows. Setting a cap below pins that lane (suspends adaptive); clearing it (0) resumes auto.
